FAQ

Do I need to own Minecraft?

Yes — Vape Client is a launcher, not a replacement for the game. You need a Microsoft account with Minecraft Java Edition.

Is Vape Client free?

Yes. The launcher is free to download and use.

Will it work with my mods?

Separate profiles keep vanilla and modded setups isolated, so your existing mod folders stay untouched.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not yet — we're focused on Windows first. Follow along for updates.
